# 🤖 Kairo — Powerful & Seamless Discord Moderation

**Kairo** is a fast, reliable, and easy-to-use Discord moderation bot designed to keep your community safe, clean, and well-managed. From basic member management to advanced safety commands, Kairo handles it all effortlessly.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🛡️ **Complete Moderation Suite:** Keep full control of your server with direct, powerful commands.
* 🔨 **Ban & Unban:** Quickly handle disruptive users and manage your server's ban list.
* 👢 **Kick:** Instantly remove problematic members with ease.
* ⏱️ **Timeout & Untimeout:** Temporarily restrict members from chatting or joining voice channels without kicking them out.
* 🔇 **Mute & Unmute:** Keep voice and text channels quiet when needed.
* 🧹 **Softban:** Easily purge recent messages while removing bad actors.

---

## 🚀 Quick Setup & Usage

1. **Invite Kairo** to your server using the official invite link.
2. Ensure **Kairo's Role** is placed above the roles of members you want to moderate.
3. Grant **Administrator** or specific moderation permissions to the bot.
4. Type `!help` (or your bot's prefix/slash command) to see all available features!

---

## 🔒 Permissions Required

To run properly, Kairo requires the following Discord permissions:
* `Ban Members`
* `Kick Members`
* `Moderate Members` (Timeout)
* `Manage Messages`
