Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kairo collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

  • User & Server IDs: Used to track moderation actions (bans, mutes, kicks, warnings).
  • Command Logs: Temporary logs of moderator command usage for audit purposes.

2. How We Use Data

The collected data is exclusively used to maintain server safety, enforce moderation rules, and provide bot functionality. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Moderation history is kept as long as the bot remains in the server. Server owners can request complete data deletion by contacting us via our support server.
